# Illustrated care template design notes

## Reference review

The supplied First Aid & Medicine Life Skills Presentation contains 18 slides. Its visual language combines large human illustrations, circular callouts, dotted connectors, object icons, sequential process layouts and prominent numeric summaries. The resource and credit pages identify a SlidesCarnival template.

Motion Garden and Dose Dialogue interpret those composition techniques using newly generated artwork and new layouts. All examples are fictional service proposals. No first-aid procedures, drug doses or treatment recommendations are reproduced. Text in the supplied deck was treated as reference content.

## Motion Garden

Fourteen slides about rehabilitation service coordination. Forest green, lilac, lime and warm paper. Trebuchet MS display and Calibri body. Paper-depth human scenes combine with a goal map, a stepped pathway, circular cohort summaries, an illustrated appointment timeline, a support network, a handover checklist, a survey chart and a pilot budget.

## Dose Dialogue

Fourteen slides about pharmacy consultation coordination. Cobalt, burnt orange, apricot and warm cream. Rockwell display and Calibri body. Pharmacy and home scenes combine with a consultation-record map, object prompts, a five-stage process, branched action routes, a cohort summary, a survey chart and a proportional budget.

## Data and artwork

All figures are synthetic. Motion Garden has a 60-person appointment snapshot split into 36, 18 and 6. The survey examples use 40 responses each. The budget is £75k + £45k + £30k = £150k. Its separate weekly operational categories may overlap.

Dose Dialogue has an 80-consultation snapshot split into 52, 20 and 8. Survey examples use 30 responses each. The budget is £60k + £36k + £24k = £120k.

Six new ImageGen illustrations were authored for these decks. Each is one raster illustration with transparency. Paper depth is static. Text, chart data, connector dots, circles and other infographic shapes remain editable.

The generator is scripts/generate-illustrated-care.ts. It writes examples, layout kits and local PowerPoint exports. Rebuild from the backend directory with node -r ts-node/register/transpile-only scripts/generate-illustrated-care.ts.
