Journey of a Coffee Bean: From Farm to Cup

An insightful look into the detailed process that transforms a humble coffee bean into your daily brew, highlighting each key step from cultivation to consumption.

🌱

Planting & Cultivation

Coffee plants, typically Arabica or Robusta, are planted in nurseries and then transferred to coffee farms, requiring specific climates and altitudes.

🍒

Harvesting Cherries

Coffee cherries are harvested, often by hand, when they are ripe and red. Selective picking ensures only the best cherries are collected.

☕

Processing Beans

The harvested cherries undergo processing to remove the outer layers. Methods include wet (washed) and dry (natural) processing, each affecting flavor.

☀️

Drying & Sorting

Processed beans are dried to reduce moisture content, then sorted by size and density to ensure uniformity in roasting.

🔥

Roasting for Flavor

Green coffee beans are roasted to develop their characteristic flavors and aromas. Roasting levels range from light to dark, each offering different taste profiles.

☕

Brewing & Enjoying

Roasted beans are ground and brewed using various methods, transforming them into a delicious cup of coffee for enjoyment.
