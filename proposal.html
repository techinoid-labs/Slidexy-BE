Overview & Executive Summary

What we aim to deliver

Design a visually compelling, highly functional mobile app using Flutter. Ensure a seamless user journey for both free and premium content consumers. Establish a monetization-ready infrastructure (ads, subscriptions, consultations). Reflect South African cultural relevance and creativity in UI/UX design.

Project Objectives

Primary goals
  • Design a modern, accessible Flutter mobile app UI/UX
  • Create a brand system for consistent identity across app & marketing
  • Implement monetization-ready flows: ads, subscriptions, in-app consultations
  • Prototype and handoff design system to development

Scope of Work

Deliverables
  • Mobile App UI/UX Design: Screens, components, interactions, onboarding flows.
  • Brand System Development: Logo, color palette, typography, iconography.
  • Prototyping: Interactive Figma/Flutter-ready prototypes for testing.
  • Design Handoff: Tokens, style guide, assets & redlines for engineers.

Project Approach

How we'll work
Discovery & Research

User interviews, market & competitor analysis, cultural context mapping (South Africa).

Wireframing & UX Flow

Low-fidelity flows to validate navigation and content architecture.

UI Design & Prototyping

High-fidelity screens, interactive prototypes, accessibility checks.
